# Think9 AI Challenge — Interactive Demo

This clickthrough follows one simulated week at a fictional apparel brand.

It shows how supplier price messages, delivery photos, call summaries and order updates could enter through one WhatsApp Business number. The sender confirms the information in the same chat before it is stored and combined with sales and stock data.

The result is a Monday action summary showing what needs ordering, what is overstocked, what needs checking and what needs no action.

## Live demo

[https://tishashahp.github.io/think9-demo/](https://tishashahp.github.io/think9-demo/)

## What is working

- Interactive clickthrough
- Runnable calculation engine included with the submission
- Stock-cover, lead-time and cash calculations generated from supplied structured data
- Monday action summary generated by the engine

## What is simulated

- Brand, vendors and commercial data
- WhatsApp screens and confirmation steps
- AI extraction from messages and images
- Live connections to WhatsApp Business API, n8n, Claude, Airtable or an ERP, and Shopify or other sales sources

All thresholds are illustrative pilot defaults, not operating recommendations.
